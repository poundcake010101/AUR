2. Information we collect

  • Order information: name, email, mobile number, shipping address, and order details.

3. Why we collect it

  • To process and fulfil your order.
  • To communicate order status and delivery updates.
  • To handle returns, exchanges, and customer support.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations.
  • To improve our website and product offering (analytics, where enabled).

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Yoco, a South African payment provider. Yoco is PCI-DSS compliant. Your card details are entered on Yoco's secure platform and are never transmitted to or stored by us. We receive only a transaction reference and payment status.

5. Shipping

We use Pargo for delivery and pickup point services across South Africa. Your name, address, and mobile number are shared with Pargo solely for the purpose of delivering your order.

6. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies and local storage strictly to operate the shopping cart. If analytics are enabled, we use a privacy-conscious analytics provider that does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. You can disable analytics by using a browser tracking blocker.

7. Data retention

Order records are retained for a minimum of five years to comply with South African tax and consumer protection law. Communications are retained for as long as needed to resolve your enquiry. Analytics data, if enabled, is retained for a maximum of 24 months.

8. Your rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information we are not legally required to retain.
  • Object to processing of your information.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us on WhatsApp at 0762734505.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), secure database access controls, and limited staff access. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Policy updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated to customers where reasonably possible.
